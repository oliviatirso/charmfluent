Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment information. We also collect photos of your teeth when provided for custom fitting purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to process and fulfil your order, communicate with you about your purchase, and improve our services. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties except as required to complete your order (e.g. payment processors, shipping providers).

Payment Processing

All payments are handled by trusted third-party processors. We do not store your full payment card details on our servers. Payment data is encrypted and processed securely.

Photos & Custom Fitting

Any dental impressions or photos you share with us are used exclusively for crafting your custom piece. These images are stored securely and are never shared publicly or used for any other purpose.

Cookies

This site may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. No personally identifiable information is stored in cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

We retain your order information for as long as necessary to provide support and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To make a request, reach out to us directly via our social channels or email.

Contact
